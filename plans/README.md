# Plans

This folder should contain active or near-active plans only.

## Active Plan Rules

- Keep templates in `plans/templates/`.
- Keep current implementation plans at top level while they are being worked.
- Move completed historical plans and reports to `docs/090-Archive/` or a future `plans/archive/`.

## Do Not Use As Source Of Truth

Older reports in `plans/reports/` are audit history. Prefer `docs/current/` for current guidance.
